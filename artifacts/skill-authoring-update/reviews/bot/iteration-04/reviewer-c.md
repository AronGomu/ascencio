# Explanation from reviewer C for candidate comprehension

## 1. Outcome and scope

Improve one required `ai-bot` against frozen opponents, including its starting AI, using reproducible paired games. Optional opponents, owner limits and a compatible checkpoint refine execution. Success delivers the accepted source, verified results and compact history; unchanged acceptance is legitimate. Only target AI changes. Decks, engine, legality and hidden-information boundaries remain protected. This explanation reads the candidate package without executing its improvement workflow.

## 2. Process overview

Prepare freezes identities and calibrates; Evaluate runs baseline or assembled candidate; Decide records acceptance; Analyze always explains that evaluation. Available decisions lead through isolated Replay and Assemble back to evaluation. Exhausted decisions or no passing edit lead to Deliver. Incidents supersede ordinary edges. The controller prints inherited policy plus one stage and validates transition names; owning tools persist evidence, while the orchestrator verifies semantic gates and authorization.
