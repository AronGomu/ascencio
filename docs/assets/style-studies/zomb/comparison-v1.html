Zomb style

The full Chapter 1 cast, one rendering language. Each comparison pairs the existing visual reference—or the written brief—with a new Zomb portrait. Click an image for a larger view. Original PNGs and full prompts are saved alongside this page.

Visual prototypes · Character canon preserved · Seven main characters plus sixteen supporting-role proposals

Zomb currently applies only to character rendering. Backgrounds and stars in these earlier studies are separate scene choices, not part of the general style.

Style source — CRT stars, iteration 6

Simplified retro 2D anime cel drawing with thin muted gray contours, broad light shapes, restrained interior detail and soft analog blur; strong fine horizontal CRT scanlines with red/cyan ghost edges and faint phosphor grain. Nearly flat matte dark surfaces with sparse low-contrast folds, smooth pale surfaces shaded faint pink. Luminous cool ivory and pale blush against near-pure black, tiny muted crimson accents and white highlights with cyan, green and red fringes. Soft even diffuse light, flattened shadow modeling, high light-dark contrast and restrained phosphor glow.
