# Fynn — protagonist

> Status: mostly defined for Chapter 1

## Identity

- Age: 15.
- Role: protagonist and player-controlled duelist.
- Family: present but off-screen in Chapter 1.
- Physique: slim, slender, average height and healthy.

## Backstory

Fynn has ordinary middle-class origins and ordinary schooling. His parents once forced him to study something else and forbade dueling for one or two months because duelists were poorly regarded. He concluded that he should never again do anything except what he feels like doing.

## Personality

Fynn is energetic, hot-headed and hungry for duels. He is lazy outside his genuine interests and does only enough schoolwork to pass. He intervenes when he sees obvious injustice. He is not a self-insert.

## Motivation

- Desire: become the best duelist in the world because he genuinely loves Duel Monsters.
- Weakness: impulsiveness, lack of discipline and neglect of anything that does not interest him.
- Need: understand that chosen effort in service of a value is not parental constraint.
- Chapter development: begin taking responsibility for the work needed to reach his goal.

What Fynn calls egoism is whim-worship: following immediate impulse instead of rationally serving a long-term value. His laziness harms his own progress, not primarily other people.

## Relationships

- Nerd: first official friend after Nerd Arc.
- Girl: investigation partner after Girl Arc.
- Rival: status-based competitive opponent.
- Senior: academy benchmark and likely final opponent.

## Duel identity

Fynn uses the player's deck. No fixed archetype is required; adaptability justifies this in fiction. A future character gives him an iconic spirit card, which is not mandatory in the deck.

He reacts strongly to unjust humiliation. A clean, clear defeat makes him depressed. Pride and a few material goods are suitable Chapter 1 stakes.

## Chapter development

Fynn enters through Qualification Arc, builds friendships and faces academy status conflict. The Nerd Arc establishes his intervention instinct and friendship. Girl Arc brings him into the missing-brother investigation and supernatural danger. Rival arcs test his response to status and demonstrated ability. Senior Finale Arc measures his progress.
