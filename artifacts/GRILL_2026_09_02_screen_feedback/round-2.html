1. Fate of the favourite ☆ toggle

The set-default star takes the top-right corner. The favourite flag still orders tiles (favourites first). If its toggle disappears, favourites become unsettable.

2. Set-default star — states and click semantics

A toggle-shaped button needs both states defined. There is always exactly one default deck per scope today; "unset default" has no meaning in the current model.
