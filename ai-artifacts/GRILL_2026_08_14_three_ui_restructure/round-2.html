1. Given the inventory, do duel files move into src/battle/ now?

"Duel files" = the six folders above, ≈127 files / 26.5k lines, imported by ~130 test files and the e2e suite.

2. Domain folder and route naming

Names appear in every import, lint rule, ticket and URL for the rest of the project.

3. What happens to the 1846-line global stylesheet?

src/styles/app.css currently mixes app reset, design tokens and duel-field rules; three domains sharing it re-creates the coupling ADR-022 removes.

4. Route table and unknown-route behavior

Deep links and refreshes must be deterministic, including mid-duel URLs.

5. What is the home hub, exactly?

You called it a testing menu; it is also the first screen a player sees, and it hosts the fullscreen notification.

6. Aspect-ratio letterboxing policy

You asked for black bars when the viewport is not full height. Where that clamp applies decides whether the accepted full-height duel geometry changes.

7. Fullscreen setting and the home-page notification

Browsers only allow fullscreen from a user gesture, so this is a control plus a persisted preference plus a dismissible prompt.

8. Deck database rename and migration failure policy

Today decks live in ygo-story-duel-deck-builder-prototype. Migration must define the new name and what happens when it fails.

9. Visual novel save storage shape

The VN prototype stores progress in a single localStorage key; a real campaign needs slots, autosave and a pre-duel checkpoint for the handoff.

10. How does a local deck legally reach the frozen engine?

Packaged card art and scripts currently cover exactly the bundled preset pool, and the Worker refuses non-bundled deck ids. A local deck can reference cards the build does not ship art for.

11. Which deck does the story opponent use?

Encounters need an opponent deck; the opponent policy is tuned against bundled preset decks.

12. Prototype removal inventory

Round 1 deletes prototype.html and reviewer specs; the reviewer tooling inside the domains is a separate call.

13. Deck editor on small viewports

The prototype shows "Desktop viewport required" below 1024px; as a production domain inside one app that becomes a visible dead end.

14. Bundle budgets after entry unification

The build verifier gates a 400 KB app closure and a 200 KB prototype closure; with one entry and lazy domains those numbers must be redefined.
