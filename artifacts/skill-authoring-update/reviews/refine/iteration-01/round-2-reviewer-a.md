# Explanation from reviewer A for prospective refinement execution

## 1. Outcome and scope

I would produce a complete, readable sibling skill package with fewer unnecessary instructions while preserving the source’s intent, dependencies, essential behavior and exact output contracts. The required input is a skill folder; optional priorities, destination, iteration count and review context shape execution. Success requires completing the requested iterations, resolving comprehension disagreements through fresh review, validating relevant changes and delivering evidence without changing or promoting the source.

## 2. Process overview

As orchestrator, I would first understand the complete incoming package and inherited authoring guidance, record its essentials and explain its execution. Each outer iteration simplifies the candidate, then enters a verification and three-reviewer loop. Corrections return through checks and fresh comprehension review until misunderstandings are resolved. I would then record counts and findings, repeat simplification for the remaining iterations and deliver the separate candidate and report. These internal changes do not initiate another authoring review.
