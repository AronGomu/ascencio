Five edges the ownership rule creates

The ownership gate turns cards into a resource decks depend on, which creates cases the feedback never had to name: selling a card a deck uses, arriving at an encounter with an illegal deck, and what each URL now means. Answer these and the frontier is empty.

Settled in round 3 — not re-asked

  • Decks live inside StoryState: saving snapshots them, loading rolls them back with the wallet and collection.
  • A new save grants a starter deck and credits its cards, so it is legal under the ownership rule and editable.
  • A story duel plays the save’s default deck, swappable at pre-battle from that save’s decks only.
  • In story mode the catalog shows owned cards only; the show-all checkbox belongs to the collection screen alone.
  • Free play answers ownership as unlimited through a mode flag — nothing materialised in storage.
  • The main menu is extracted into the shell, story-styled; the story domain stays lazy; #/duel becomes #/free-play.
  • Free play gets an opponent picker as well as the deck picker.
  • One plan, every ticket in dependency order.
1. What happens when you sell a card one of that save’s decks uses?

The sell screen is the only place a collection shrinks, so it is the only place the “every card in a deck is owned” invariant can break.

2. What happens if the chosen deck is not legal at an encounter?

A save deck can be under 40 cards, or (depending on question 1) reference cards you sold. The pre-battle screen is the last point before the engine loads a deck it will reject.

3. What is the full route table after the menu move?

The editor now serves two contexts, and a deep link has to say which one — or refuse. Settings and the admin console also lose their current home when the shell home screen goes.

4. What can the free-play opponent picker choose from?

You asked for an opponent picker on top of the deck picker. Its source list decides whether free play is a demo or a sandbox.

5. Confirm the starting grant for a new story save

There is exactly one starter deck in the repo (src/battle/duel/presets/decks/player.ydk, seeded today by ensureStarterDeck), and a new save currently starts with 1000 DP. Confirm or override before it is written into a ticket.
