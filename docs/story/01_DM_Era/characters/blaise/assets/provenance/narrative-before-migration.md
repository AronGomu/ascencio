# Blaise — childhood friend

> Status: name Blaise retained by the owner; short-haired gothic appearance selected

## Identity

- Role: Finn's childhood friend and December arrival.
- History: knew Finn before Violet Academy.
- Rank: Red in the current visual direction.

## Backstory and motivation

Blaise's parents initially prevented her from following Finn to Violet Academy because they opposed a duelist or conflict-profession path. She began another course of adult education, but separation from Finn made her increasingly unhappy. She eventually confronted her parents and changed direction.

She is secretly in love with Finn. She does not fully admit this motive to herself and hides it behind plausible explanations: learning the campus, providing Finn with a competent training partner, joining an interesting investigation or organizing him because he cannot manage alone.

Her central contradiction is that she had the courage to change her life to rejoin Finn but not yet the courage to tell him why.

## Personality

Blaise is energetic, active, socially forceful and habitually organizing something. She combines a childhood friend's natural closeness with light tsundere deflection, busybody involvement, harmless koakuma-style mischief and a gap between gothic styling and a bright temperament. She does not hide her energy; she hides the person toward whom it is mainly directed.

## Appearance

Blaise is an adult woman with a slim adult silhouette with the owner-selected literal bust circumference of 40 cm. Her design uses the original CRT-study character: a chin-length pale ivory bob with faint pink or silver shading, long bangs, red eyes and the selected black blushing spider hair clip (round 3, option 20), with ivory dot eyes, pink cheeks and a pink bow. Her slightly feline eyes and restrained mischievous smile contrast with her energetic personality.

Her current casual design wears a black off-shoulder knit top, a dark pleated skirt with web trim, opaque tights and ankle boots. Casual fabric may retain spiderweb styling. All outfits omit the spider pendant; the choker is plain. Her academic Red uniforms have plain tailoring and trim without spider/web motifs, pins or necklace charms; the selected spider hair clip remains. The owner selected this design in place of the former long-pink-haired academy-uniform studies. Her academy rank remains Red; summer and winter academy-uniform adaptations are available as the outfit studies below.
