# Zomb style

Character-rendering style derived from the CRT stars study, iteration 6. Named Zomb by the owner.

Scope: character designs only for now. Backgrounds, scenery, stars, decorative motifs, and scene composition are outside this style. The source study's black backdrop and phosphor stars are not Zomb style requirements.

Art style : Simplified retro 2D anime cel character drawing with thin muted gray contours, broad light shapes, restrained interior detail and soft analog blur; strong fine horizontal CRT scanlines with red/cyan ghost edges and faint phosphor grain applied to the character rendering.
Fashion / material rendering : Nearly flat matte dark surfaces with sparse low-contrast folds, fine mesh strokes and smooth pale surfaces shaded with faint pink.
Color palette : Luminous cool ivory and pale blush character highlights contrasted with dark clothing and shadows; restrained crimson accents and subtle cyan, green and red fringes along character contours. Preserve each character's established skin tone, hair color, and uniform rank colors. No background palette is prescribed.
Lighting : Soft even diffuse light, flattened shadow modeling, high light-dark contrast and restrained phosphor glow.

Source: [iteration 6 general prompt](../crt-stars-2026-10-28/general_6.txt), retained unchanged as historical input. This definition includes the owner's later scope clarification.

The owner selected the 23 regenerated-v2 cast designs as the current visual references, recorded in docs/story/themes/current-character-designs.yaml. Earlier experiments remain historical. Written character canon remains authoritative for story facts. Preserve individual character identities and uniform rank colors while applying the shared rendering treatment.

The original study images and their saved generation prompts predate this clarification. Their backgrounds are study-specific choices, not reusable style instructions. The regenerated-v2 designs follow this character-only definition, using a plain neutral review backdrop as a separate presentation choice.
