# Asset rules

## Complete outfit sets

First appearance in an outfit requires the complete planned sprite set for that outfit, not only the expressions needed by its first scene.

Story planning tracks:

- New characters.
- New outfits.
- Seasonal outfit changes.
- Backgrounds.
- Special CGs.
- Duel-specific assets.
- Map and environment variants.

## Seasonal continuity

Chapter 1 covers one school year, so recurring characters need complete sprite sets for every outfit and season in which they appear:

- Late summer.
- Autumn.
- Winter.
- Spring and early summer.

Seasonal progression also changes outdoor backgrounds, academy map visuals, weather and environmental details. Winter backgrounds may include snow.
