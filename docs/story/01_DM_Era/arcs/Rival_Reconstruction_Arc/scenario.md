# Rival Reconstruction Arc — scenario

> Status: planned; placement undecided

## Purpose

Show Rival rebuilding dueling competence and personal identity without erasing his pride or abrasive personality.

## Beats

- Rival trains after withdrawing socially.
- He asks why he actually lost instead of blaming luck.
- He returns and challenges Fynn to test himself.
- Third duel can be won or lost.
- If Rival wins, he is genuinely pleased and may gloat.
- If Rival loses, he accepts the result and plans to improve.
- Either result shows confidence grounded in effort rather than status.

## Canon lines

> Rival: Damn. I really got better. I proved myself.

> Rival: Fine. I'll get better for next time.
