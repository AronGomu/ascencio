Locked decisions

Board floor wins

min-width:52rem and 44px targets never yield. Preview/field internal pan absorbs pressure.

Deck tests

Structural deck/pool/manifest/picker tests automated. No matchup tests. User plays one duel per archetype.

Motion

No animation dep. One hand-rolled rAF ghost. prefers-reduced-motion has zero tilt/spring/zoom.

Selection

Any exact minimum===maximum===1 target submits. Multi uses counter + Confirm.
