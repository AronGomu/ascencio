# Atticus — Alexis's missing brother

> Status: provisional name; Chapter 1 history and outcome defined

## Identity

- Role: Alexis's older brother and central Chapter 1 mystery.
- Status: alive but absorbed and controlled by the Ténèbres before his rescue.
- Social position: charismatic former Violet Academy student and gifted duelist.

## Personality

Atticus is pleasant, intuitive, playful, socially magnetic and naturally talented. He is athletic, relaxed and accustomed to people liking him. His growing pride remains mostly internal and does not immediately make him cruel.

Repeated losses to Zen become intolerable because they prove that natural talent has a limit. Atticus does not openly hate Zen; he cannot accept that another duelist remains beyond him.

## Disappearance

Ascensio's world has modern or slightly advanced internet and artificial-intelligence technology. Atticus finds occult archives, esoteric communities and digitized books online. His intuitive, experimental temperament leads him to try a ritual before understanding its consequences.

He begins believing his mind can directly act on reality. That belief makes him receptive to the Ténèbres and attracts Darkness. Seeking enough power to defeat Zen, Atticus contacts the organization, loses a duel against its agents and is absorbed.

His jealousy, impatience and search for a shortcut led him to Darkness, so he is not a wholly passive victim. After absorption, however, he becomes the prisoner of a force beyond him.

## Appearance

Atticus is a handsome adult blond man with a square masculine face and medium-length hair stopping above the shoulders. He is clean-shaven. His normal presence is that of an American golden boy: bright, athletic, relaxed, charming and naturally comfortable in a group.

He wears the university uniform much more loosely than other students, with an open blazer or loose polo, relaxed collar, rolled sleeves and hands in pockets. This comes from temperament rather than status display.

Under the Ténèbres' control, a mask covers all or part of his face or head. His clothing becomes entirely black and his silhouette rigid, closed and threatening. The exact mask and outfit remain undecided. The design must contrast his ordinary warmth with a dehumanized absence of openness.

## Chapter development

Alexis first believes he may still be found. A Darkness contact later convinces the trio that he is permanently lost. Near the end of the chapter, they discover him alive but conditioned or controlled. Finn, Sirius and Alexis defeat him in a duel whose purpose is liberation, and genuinely rescue him.
