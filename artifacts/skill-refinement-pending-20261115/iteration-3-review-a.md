# Explanation from reviewer A for fresh comprehension

## 1. Outcome and scope

Improve one supplied target AI through reproducible paired games against frozen opponents, including its starting AI. Deliver the accepted source, verified results and compact history; unchanged acceptance is a valid outcome. Only target AI changes; decks, engine, legality and hidden-information boundaries remain protected. Optional opponents, owner limits and compatible checkpoints determine setup.

## 2. Process overview

Prepare pins identities, policy, limits and two calibrations. Evaluate baseline or combined candidate, Decide, then Analyze even the final/rejected evaluation. If authorized, Replay isolated rules, Assemble passing edits and repeat evaluation. No qualifying rule or the decision ceiling leads to delivery; exhaustion suspends. The controller checks legal edges, while project tools persist execution and the orchestrator verifies semantic gates.

| Pending ledger | Every reason, including singleton | Explicit aliases; distinct corrections get distinct IDs | Provenance, semantic rule/source hashes, loss/decision/choice identities | Orchestrator persists bindings; tools do not automate ledger |

Promotion uses equal-opponent weighted game win rates on completed paired intersections; draws score zero, either-side ignores exclude both sides, and every opponent needs a pair. Ties, regressions and unassessable results retain acceptance.
