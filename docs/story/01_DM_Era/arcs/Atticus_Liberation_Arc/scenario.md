# Third Alexis Arc — Atticus's liberation

> Status: planned; late Chapter 1

## Purpose

Reveal that Atticus survived, confront the consequences of his choices and complete Alexis's emotional progression through an act of rescue.

## Beats

- The group discovers evidence that contradicts Atticus's supposed final loss.
- They find him alive but absorbed, conditioned or controlled by the Ténèbres.
- His behavior can make it appear that he willingly joined the occult forces.
- The truth preserves his responsibility for seeking power while establishing that his current state is captivity.
- Finn, Sirius and Alexis face him in a duel.
- Their objective is liberation rather than punishment or destruction.
- Victory breaks the Ténèbres' control and genuinely rescues Atticus.

## Open continuity

The conditioning mechanism, controlled appearance, duel format, stakes and exact means by which victory frees Atticus remain open.
