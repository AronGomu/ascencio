# The Ténèbres and Darkness

> Status: provisional cosmology and organization concept

## Vocabulary

- **The Ténèbres** are a Lovecraftian entity or phenomenon.
- **Darkness** is the human organization that studies, invokes or attempts to exploit it.

These names and the rules below remain provisional. Chapter 1 may establish their effects before it establishes a complete explanation.

## The Ténèbres

The current model is that collective imagination, belief and fear nourish the Ténèbres. Rituals and mysterious manifestations create contact between human belief and physical reality. As belief accumulates, the entity can produce tangible effects such as absorption, disappearance, mental control, altered duels and materialized phenomena.

Practitioners believe they are drawing on an external power. They may instead be helping create or materialize the force that harms them.

Ascensio's objectivist foundation suggests that a person who refuses to treat imagination as a creator of reality may be harder for the Ténèbres to influence. It is not yet established whether disbelief grants immunity or only resistance. This cannot become a convenient escape from consequences; its limits require explicit rules.

## Darkness

Darkness operates clandestinely behind a modern corporate facade. It has legitimate activities, institutional resources and access to scientific and technological expertise, while secretly diverting part of those resources into occult research.

The organization uses ranks, initiations, symbols, guarded knowledge and ritualized internal practices. Its agents include the mysterious people encountered during Alexis's investigation, and its experiments can produce real manifestations of the Ténèbres.

Its fictional visual language may draw broadly from initiatory societies and Masonic symbolism. It does not represent real Freemasonry or attribute Darkness's actions to it.

## Open definition

The following remain undecided:

- Darkness's definitive name, hierarchy, objectives and corporate facade.
- Its symbols, ranks, rituals and agents.
- The exact conditions under which belief strengthens the Ténèbres.
- Ritual costs and limits.
- The relationship between the Ténèbres, cards and duels.
