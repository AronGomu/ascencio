# First Darkness agent — mysterious opponent

> Status: identity and name undefined

## Role

The agent is connected to a lead in Alexis's investigation. She eventually meets and duels him alone. He defeats her before Finn and Sirius intervene.

Finn then defeats him in a mandatory Best of 3. Before the group can question him or obtain more information, the Ténèbres absorb or consume him. His disappearance proves that the threat can act materially while preserving the mystery around Atticus.

The agent belongs to or works with Darkness, the human organization described in [`../../00_Global_Scenario/darkness.md`](../../00_Global_Scenario/darkness.md).
