# Concept

> Status: working draft — Chapter 1 in design

A Yu-Gi-Oh! fan game with **100% original** characters and story, with no canon link to the official universe. A parallel universe.

**Tone and gameplay references:** the older Yu-Gi-Oh! games — *Spirit Caller*, *The Duelists of the Roses*, *Nightmare Troubadour*, the *World Championship* series. The scenario has to generate credible, regular pretexts for playable duels.

## Chapter structure

The game is cut into chapters. **One chapter = one Yu-Gi-Oh! era.**

| Chapter | Era | Mechanic introduced |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Duel Monsters | Fusion, Ritual |
| 2 | GX | — |
| 3 | 5D's | Synchro |
| 4 | ZEXAL | Xyz |
| 5 | ARC-V | Pendulum |
| 6 | VRAINS | Link |
| … | … | … |

Each chapter:

- introduces the mechanics of its era
- raises the depth and complexity of the story by one notch
- has to work as a **standalone game**
