1. How is this feedback split into plans and branches?

Branch ownership is a hard rule (duel → src/battle/, vn → src/story/, deck → src/deck-editor/), and the VN file ends with a deck-builder feature, so one feedback file no longer maps to one branch.

2. Duel 5 — what order does your own hand render in after a search?

The searched card is not appended at the right because ocgcore shuffles your hand after a search (MSG_SHUFFLE_HAND) and the projector adopts that order; the fix is a display policy, and it must not leak anything (your own hand is fully known to you anyway).

3. Duel 9 — what exactly does “restore the last non-bugged state” restore?

ocgcore has no state snapshot in the vendored build, so the only honest rewind is a deterministic replay of the recorded response log against the same seed — which means deciding where the replay stops and who answers next.

4. Duel 6 — when does a drag-and-drop open a confirmation menu?

Today a drop auto-picks one action per zone (drop-target.ts: activate beats set, summon beats set-monster). The feedback wants the choice surfaced — the question is whether that is spells only or every ambiguous drop.

5. Duel 4 — what does clicking a hand card do when only one action is legal?

Click currently fires immediately when there is exactly one choice and only opens a menu when there are several (DuelField.svelte:431). The requested “click freezes the zoom, orange halo, buttons stay” model has to say what happens in the one-choice case.

6. Duel 8 — how wide is the engine-response fix?

The announce-number bug is one line, but it is a whole class of bug: every prompt answer either sends an index, a raw value, or a card code, and one wrong pick kills a duel with no diagnosis. Tick as many as you want.

7. VN shop 1 — where do the set images come from?

“Fetch the original images for each set” needs a source, and the app is offline-first with hash-verified, pinned assets — 50 set images is a new asset pipeline unless we reuse what is already bundled.

8. VN reveal 5 — when do bought cards enter the collection?

Cards are credited at open time today, and pack price (150 DP) was deliberately set to kill open-and-sell arbitrage; crediting at purchase changes when the pack contents are rolled and what an unopened pack is worth.

9. VN reveal 1 — pacing and persistence of the auto-flip option

“A checkbox that automates the flips” needs a speed and a memory; it also decides whether the Hearthstone-style click reveal is the default.

10. VN — which preview surface do the shop screens reuse?

“Same card preview from duels and decks” is the shell CardPreviewPanel (ADR-036), but the reveal screen asks for a floating window beside the zoomed card while the card list is a browsing grid — those want different placements of the same component.

11. Collection feature — who owns the data and how far does the viewer go?

The collection lives in the story save today (StoryState.collection, ADR-033) but the viewer is asked for in the deck-builder menu, so the two domains have to meet at a public contract — and “can I only build decks from cards I own” is a much bigger change than a browse screen.

12. VN general 4 & 5 — where does the shared choice component live?

Narrative choices, the shopkeeper menu and any future multiple choice are asked to share one centred component, and cancel/back actions are asked to be red — both are either story-local or a shell-wide primitive, and that decides which branch they land on.
