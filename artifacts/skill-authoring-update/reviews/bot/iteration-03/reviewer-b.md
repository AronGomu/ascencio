# Explanation from reviewer B for prospective bot improvement execution

## 1. Outcome and scope

Given one required target AI package, optional opponents, owner limits and possibly a compatible checkpoint, I would seek a strictly better paired-game win rate and deliver accepted source, verified results and compact history. Only target AI may change. Decks, legality, engine and information boundaries remain protected. Successful completion can honestly retain unchanged source when no edit qualifies; suspension remains incomplete. This explanation describes execution without running that workflow.

## 2. Process overview

I would resolve configuration, archive starting source and calibrate separate match/replay pools, then evaluate baseline. Every evaluation reaches a persisted decision and complete loss analysis, including the final candidate. Eligible hypotheses become isolated edits tested through exact-decision replays. Passing edits assemble into another evaluated candidate until the decision ceiling or no qualifying edit. Stops interrupt this loop through checkpointed suspension; authorized resume preserves identities and consumption. The stateless controller validates stage edges while owning scripts persist work.
