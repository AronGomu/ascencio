# Explanation from readable_i5_b for independent comprehension review

## 1. Outcome and scope

Improve supplied `ai-bot` against frozen opponents including its starting self, delivering accepted source. Only target AI changes; decks, legality, engine and hidden information remain protected. No qualifying edit retains accepted source. Use `session-typescript/v1`; publishing requires separate authorization.

## 2. Process overview

Prepare frozen identities, effective limits, analysis policy and both calibration recommendations; evaluate baseline, decide its reference, then analyze. Repeatedly replay isolated corrections, assemble passing corrections, evaluate and decide candidates, and analyze even the final rejection. The stateless controller checks transitions; tools persist checkpoints; the orchestrator verifies gates. No passing rule or decision ceiling leads to delivery; incidents suspend.

## 8. Ambiguities and interpretations

After two acceptances, Replay finding no qualifying edit sends Deliver the second accepted version; it does not restore the starting bot (Constraints: retain accepted source; Replay: none retains accepted; Deliver: export accepted). Unsupported current-policy reconstruction blocks a rule. Pending stacks grant no budget; discarded campaigns transfer none without authorization.
