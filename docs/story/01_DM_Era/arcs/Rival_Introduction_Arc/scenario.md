# Rival Introduction Arc — scenario

> Status: defined at high level; order undecided

## Purpose

Introduce status conflict and establish Rival as more than a generic bully.

## Beats

- Rival Goon A challenges Fynn in a single-game duel; either result continues the story.
- If Goon A loses, he reports the result to Rival.
- Rival Goon B challenges Fynn, dismissing him as "just a Red" and blaming Goon A's incompetence.
- A public incident humiliates, mistreats or unfairly dismisses a Red student.
- Fynn intervenes because he hates obvious injustice.
- Rival reacts according to the prior goon results but confronts Fynn in every branch.
- Fynn wins a mandatory Best of 3 against Rival.
- Rival rationalizes the defeat as luck, matchup, underestimation or preparation failure.

## Branch invariant

The public incident and Rival confrontation work after 2–0, 1–1 or 0–2 goon results. Dialogue changes; progression does not.
