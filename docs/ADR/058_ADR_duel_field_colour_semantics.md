# ADR-058: Selection Reads as a Colour Change, Not a Shape Change; Orange Widens to "the Current Locus"

> Status: accepted; planned
> Decided: 2026-08-27
> Owners: field presentation architecture
> Amends: ADR-015 §Decision (selected/drop-candidate rows), the `.duel-field-zone.is-actionable` / `.duel-field-card.is-actionable .duel-field-card__art` / `.duel-field-stack.is-actionable` treatment, and the invariant comment at `src/styles/app.css:1386-1393`

## Context

ADR-015 fixed green to "legal/actionable" and orange to "selected," and `src/styles/app.css:1386-1393` states that invariant in a comment: "Green is the 'you may act here' colour across the field. Orange is reserved for selection below: selection is a different state from legality and the two must never be confusable." The rule was written for one prompt family and has since been applied uniformly to two.

`src/battle/app/prompts/interaction-spec.ts:136-139` maps four selection prompts — `selectCard`, `selectTribute`, `selectSum`, `selectUnselectCard` — to interaction kind `"cardSelection"`, distinct from `"cardAction"` (`interaction-spec.ts:130-135`). Both kinds currently render the same green treatment: `.duel-field-zone.is-actionable` and `.duel-field-card.is-actionable .duel-field-card__art` paint a border-color of `var(--success)` plus a two-layer box-shadow — `0 0 0 3px color-mix(in srgb, var(--legal) 90%, transparent)` and `0 0 10px color-mix(in srgb, var(--legal) 40%, transparent)` — at `src/styles/app.css:1395-1401`, and `.duel-field-stack.is-actionable` repeats it at `1403-1408`. `--legal` and `--success` both resolve to `#7ee2a8`; `--selected` and `--warning` both resolve to `#ffd580` (`src/styles/tokens.css:41-44`). The comment above the rule (`app.css:1389-1393`) explains this was deliberate: a near-opaque ring plus a spread-free glow, chosen so a rotated card (defense, set) does not leave a hard-edged band standing inside its own upright box — but at field scale on a `cardSelection` prompt the effect still reads as a card filled solid green, because there is no fill/outline distinction between "you may activate this" and "you may pick this."

The active player's identity block gets a separate treatment: `.duel-right-rail__identity.active` sets `border-color: var(--accent)` (`app.css:565-566`) and its avatar `img` carries a permanent `border: 4px solid var(--accent)` (`app.css:584`) — teal, not orange, and not conditioned on which seat is active for the border-4px rule.

The owner's 2026-08-27 feedback round, discussing this exact screen, asked for: dashed green borders on valid selection targets that turn dashed orange once selected, replacing the green fill and the `"Select"` chip (`src/battle/app/presentation/card-action-label.ts:29`); an orange halo on actionable piles; and orange avatar/life-point borders for the active player with grey for the inactive one. That request treats orange as answering one question — "what is live right now" — across selection, piles, and turn identity, rather than meaning "selected card" alone.

## Decision

1. **Selection-family prompts render candidates with a dashed outline, no fill.** For interaction kind `"cardSelection"` (`selectCard`, `selectSum`, `selectUnselectCard`, `selectTribute`), a candidate is a dashed `--legal` (green) outline with no background fill and no glow. Selecting it changes the outline colour to dashed `--selected` (orange); it does not change from dashed to solid, and it does not gain a fill. Selection is a colour change on the same dashed shape, never a shape change.
2. **`cardAction` prompts keep the existing solid green legality ring unchanged.** The `.duel-field-zone.is-actionable` / `.duel-field-card.is-actionable .duel-field-card__art` / `.duel-field-stack.is-actionable` solid-ring-plus-glow treatment at `app.css:1395-1408` continues to apply only to interaction kind `"cardAction"`. The two prompt families are now visually distinguishable by border style — dashed for "you are being asked to choose among these," solid for "you may act on this" — where before they were identical.
3. **Actionable piles (stacks) get an orange halo, not a green one.** `.duel-field-stack.is-actionable` moves from the shared green treatment to an orange (`--selected`) halo, because a pile offered as a legal choice is answering "what are you being asked about," which clause 4 below groups with orange rather than with `cardAction` legality.
4. **Orange now carries three meanings, unified as one concept.** Selected card (clause 1), actionable-pile halo (clause 3), and active-player identity — avatar border and life-point border both move from teal `--accent` to `--selected` for the active player and to a grey/neutral tone for the inactive one, replacing the always-teal, always-4px avatar border at `app.css:584` and the `--accent` active-identity border at `app.css:565-566`. All three answer the same question: "the current locus of the turn or the choice."
5. **`app.css:1386-1393` is corrected in the same commit that changes the rules below it.** The comment is rewritten to state the revised invariant — green marks a `cardAction` legal target or a dashed selection candidate; orange marks a selected candidate, an actionable pile, or the active player — so the comment and the CSS it sits above never again diverge, per the standing rule that a stale invariant comment is worse than none.

## Consequences

- Orange is overloaded three ways and no longer identifies one concept at a glance. Accepted: all three uses answer "what is live right now," and the owner asked for this consolidation specifically.
- A reader who learned "green means legal" from ADR-015 will, on first seeing a dashed green selection candidate, expect it to be directly activatable — it is not; it is a choice candidate. The border style (dashed) is the only thing distinguishing it from a `cardAction` target, and a border style is a weaker signal than hue was under ADR-015.
- The distinction between `cardSelection` and `cardAction` prompts now lives entirely in dashed-versus-solid, not in colour. Any future prompt kind that reuses solid-green styling without checking `interactionKind` silently collapses back into the pre-ADR-058 ambiguity this ADR removes.
- The inactive player's identity border loses its always-on teal accent; a reader relying on `var(--accent)` to mean "this is a player identity block" (rather than "this is the active player") will need to re-learn the association.
- `--accent` (`#73daca`) stops being used for turn identity in the duel rail; it remains defined in `src/styles/tokens.css:39` and in use elsewhere (deck editor tile borders, story screens) — this decision narrows its duel-field usage only, not the token itself.

## Alternatives rejected

- **Keep green for selection, use a third hue for the pile halo.** Rejected: the owner's request named orange specifically for the pile halo, and introducing a fourth semantic colour (green/orange/teal/new) would add a colour to learn without removing the fill-reads-as-solid-card complaint that motivated this round.
- **Recolour everything orange, including `cardAction` legality.** Rejected: this collapses the two prompt families (ADR-015's "legal" vs "selected" split, and this ADR's `cardAction` vs `cardSelection` split) into one colour, which is the exact ambiguity ADR-015 was written to remove. Border style alone was judged sufficient to carry a two-way split; it was not judged sufficient to carry a one-colour, three-way split.
- **Keep the green fill and only add dashes.** Rejected: the owner's complaint was specifically that the solid fill reads as a card filled solid green at field scale, particularly on rotated (defense/set) cards where the glow's spread-free design (see `app.css:1389-1393`) still leaves the ring reading as a filled box. Adding dashes on top of a fill does not remove the fill; the fill itself is what has to go for `cardSelection` targets.
