# Explanation from reviewer-b for candidate comprehension

## 1. Outcome and scope

Given a skill folder, produce a complete, readable sibling refinement preserving its intent, dependencies, essential behavior and output contracts. Optional priorities, destination and iteration count guide execution; five iterations is the default. Successful completion includes reviewed candidate files and external evidence covering every iteration. The source remains read-only, and promotion requires a later separate user instruction. This document describes execution without performing it.

## 2. Process overview

The orchestrator reads the package and inherited authoring guidance, inventories behavior, and explains the incoming skill. It then simplifies the candidate, validates relevant changes, and commissions three independent comprehension explanations. Comparison drives minimal repairs and another verification/review round until misunderstandings disappear. That inner loop precedes the next outer simplification iteration; completing the requested outer count leads to delivery, without recursive authoring reviews for candidate changes.

| Incident | Immediate action | Retry allowance | Evidence preserved | Resume condition |
