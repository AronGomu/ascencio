# Explanation from reviewer B for prospective skill authoring

## 1. Outcome and scope

I would create or revise the requested skill, preserving useful existing instructions and the user's scope, then deliver that authored package plus a separately reviewed refinement candidate. Required inputs are the intended task and sufficient source material to identify its behavior. Success includes usable instructions, explicit model defaults, relevant verification, and the automatic five-iteration review for each changed skill. This document explains execution only; it performs none of those operations.

## 2. Process overview

I would read the ordered guidelines and supporting writing guidance, resolve intent, inventory obligations, then adapt the template to the actual task. After checking the completed authoring work, I would dispatch the refinement review with the specified context and settings. That review establishes its own baseline, simplifies, verifies, and repeatedly obtains three fresh comprehension explanations until misunderstandings are resolved, then proceeds through five iterations. Candidate work remains within that review rather than starting another authoring review.

## 3. Execution matrix

| Step | Entry condition | Action and owner | Concurrency | Decision/completion criterion | Saved output | Next step |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Authoring requested | Author reads guidelines, writing guidance and mechanics; identifies scope and requirements | No required parallelism | Inputs and behavioral essentials identified | Requirement inventory for revisions | 2 |
| 2 | Intent understood | Author fills applicable template sections, preserves contracts, removes comments and placeholders | Independent checks may overlap | Observable goal, actionable workflow and completion gates are clear | Authored skill and needed resources | 3 |
| 3 | Draft complete | Author checks references, affected literal contracts and relevant executable behavior | As supported | Requirements retained; changes separately identified | Validation evidence | 4 |
| 4 | Authoring complete | Author spawns refinement child with source, separate destination, settings and review context | Child may delegate reviewers | Child receives complete review assignment | Review candidate | 5 |
| 5 | Candidate edited | Child verifies, obtains three fresh explanations, compares by source step and matrix item, corrects misunderstandings | Three independent reviewers | No behavioral misunderstanding remains | Explanations and findings outside candidate | Repeat simplification through iteration five |
| 6 | Five iterations complete | Author delivers authored result and child findings honestly | None required | Counts, links, checks and limitations reported | Final report | Await separate promotion instruction |

## 4. Identities and invariants

| Item | Initial source/value | Allowed changes | Must remain fixed | Verification |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Authored package | Completed authoring output | Later separately authorized work | Read-only throughout refinement | Compare source identity/content |
| Behavior baseline | Existing requirements and explicit owner decisions | Authorized behavioral changes recorded separately | Contracts, ownership, thresholds and failure distinctions | Deletion audit and boundary walkthroughs |
| Candidate | Complete unique sibling package | Review corrections | Separate from source and external review artifacts | Destination/reference checks |
| Guidance | Candidate authoring rules for circular dependency | Routine interpretation under priority order | Earlier guideline rows prevail | Resolve conflicts against ordered table |

## 5. Limits and accounting

| Resource/stage | Default limit | Override rule | Accounting | Action at exhaustion |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Model/thinking | GPT-6.1 Sol, low | Explicit user settings or documented task override | Report actual/unavailable controls | Leave unavailable required review pending |
| Automatic review | Five iterations per changed skill | Explicit session instructions govern | Counts and findings each iteration | Deliver after completion |
| Comprehension review | Three fresh reviewers per round | Required delegation must be available | Compare normalized decisions, not prose | Fix and repeat while misunderstandings remain |
| Reduction | Aspirational 25% | Readability and preservation win | Count all instructional resources, including relocated/generated copies; separate physical size | No forced destructive reduction |

## 6. Incidents and continuation

| Incident | Immediate action | Retry allowance | Evidence preserved | Resume condition |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Missing essential intent | Ask only blocking clarification | No invented quota | Known requirements and conflict | Owner resolves behavioral choice |
| Failed check or misunderstanding | Correct smallest responsible issue | Review loop has no numerical retry cap | Check failures and reviewer explanations | Verification and fresh comprehension succeed |
| Existing candidate | Select unique sibling | No overwrite attempt needed | Existing package | Safe destination chosen |
| Delegation/settings unavailable | Report review pending | No substitute success claim | Actual controls and completed work | Required review becomes possible |

## 7. Delivery and retention

|---|---|---|---|---|
| Authored skill | Applicable template, explicit defaults, necessary resources | Requested authoring destination | Remains source after delivery | No automatic replacement authorized |
| Candidate | Complete refined package | Separate sibling | Review and delivery; existing candidates preserved | No automatic cleanup specified |
| Review evidence/report | Explanations, counts, findings, checks, limitations | Outside candidate | Supports assessment and continuation | No blanket deletion permission |
