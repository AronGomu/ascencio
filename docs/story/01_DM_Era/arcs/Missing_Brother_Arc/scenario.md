# Second Alexis Arc — false conclusion

> Status: planned; current position after Chaz's second arc

## Purpose

Turn Alexis's renewed hope into a credible apparent ending, then give her grief enough narrative space before Atticus's survival is revealed.

## Beats

- Alexis shares new information instead of investigating alone.
- Finn, Sirius and Alexis follow the lead as a group.
- They encounter a second Darkness agent or someone directly connected to the first.
- A duel ends in the group's victory.
- The contact explains that Atticus pursued occult practices, was swallowed by the Ténèbres and can never return.
- The contact may sincerely believe this account; the falsehood comes from incomplete knowledge rather than a required lie.
- Alexis accepts that Atticus is dead or permanently irrecoverable.
- The arc ends with grief and the apparent disappearance of the hope that drove her investigation.

## Boundaries

The audience should be able to believe the conclusion temporarily. The arc does not reveal Atticus alive or immediately reverse Alexis's grief.
