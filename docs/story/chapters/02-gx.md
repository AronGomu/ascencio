# Chapter 2 — GX

> Status: noted for later. Era mechanics: none introduced.

- Fynn applies an **altruist** morality: he effaces himself, ignores his own needs, sacrifices himself continually for the friends of chapter 1
- He observes that this path leads to his own destruction
- He draws the lesson and consciously chooses **rational egoism**

> The fame won in chapter 1 is exactly the mechanism that makes altruism possible and destructive in chapter 2: everyone comes to claim something from the one who has value. **Chapter 1 builds chapter 2's trap.** The rule "a duel cannot be refused" turns his fame into a permanent target.
