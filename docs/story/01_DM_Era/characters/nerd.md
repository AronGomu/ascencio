# Nerd — analyst and friend

> Status: strongly defined for Chapter 1

## Identity

- Role: Fynn's main friend and central trio member.
- Family: younger brother of the Senior, who is two years older.
- Physique: short and small in Chapter 1.

## Backstory

The Senior has always been confident, decisive, capable and reliable under pressure. He handled everything while the Nerd grew up in his shadow. The Nerd does not deeply resent him; constant comparison damaged his confidence.

## Personality

The Nerd is insecure, avoidant and curious. He avoids confrontation, delays difficult decisions, lets more capable people act and escapes situations exposing weakness. Unanswered questions bother him. He investigates people, decks and mysteries because he wants information, not automatically from courage or altruism.

## Duel identity

He is not an exceptional pilot. Under pressure he hesitates, overthinks, makes avoidable mistakes and often sees the correct line too late. His natural talent is metagame understanding:

- Deck building.
- Card evaluation.
- Matchup theory.
- Statistics.
- Metagame prediction.
- Strategy analysis.
- Weakness identification.

> Senior: Give me a deck and I'll pilot it better than you.
>
> Nerd: Give me a format and I'll understand it better than you.

## Chapter development

During Nerd Arc, three classmates or bullies take a meaningful card after defeating him. Fynn intervenes. The Nerd later hides in his room after missing class. Fynn forces him to act through a Best of 3 duel. He returns to class and becomes Fynn's friend, but does not become confident overnight.

Long-term development: a Chapter 2 growth spurt makes him taller than Fynn while his internal identity remains small. Later he becomes a better duelist and fully values theory, construction and analysis without measuring himself against the Senior.
