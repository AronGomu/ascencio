## Violet Academy uniform

The uniform is simple and close to a conventional Japanese university uniform. Red, Yellow and Blue indicate rank. Every polo and blazer carries the Violet Academy's embroidered crest.

Summer uniform:

- A rank-colored polo with white collar and sleeve trim.
- Dark shorts, ordinary socks and simple low shoes for men.
- A dark pleated skirt with a rank-colored lower stripe, opaque tights and simple low shoes for women.

Winter uniform:

- A white shirt and simple rank-colored blazer with the crest.
- A restrained tie or ribbon where appropriate.
- Dark trousers and classic low shoes for men.
- A dark pleated skirt with a rank-colored stripe, opaque tights and classic low shoes for women.

Students may personalize how they wear the uniform while preserving its immediately recognizable base. Chaz wears it with calculated informality. Atticus wears it very loosely. Alexis follows the rules precisely. Blaise uses the selected short-haired ivory/pink bob and black blushing spider hair clip. Her casual clothes retain gothic web details and a pendant-free choker. Her academic uniforms use plain Red tailoring, shirts/polos and ribbons without spider/web motifs; the hair clip remains. Earlier ornate academic and long-pink-haired studies are historical.

Long coats, heavy chains, numerous straps and elaborate fantasy accessories from early experiments are not the canonical uniform.

## Illustration direction

Character rendering uses [Zomb style](../../assets/style-studies/zomb/Zomb-style.md): simplified retro anime cel shapes, thin muted contours, nearly flat matte materials, restrained soft shading, CRT scanlines and subtle color ghosting along character contours. Preserve distinct silhouettes and character-specific palettes. Stars, scenery and background colors are excluded from the general style; the gray gallery backdrop is only a review presentation.

An alternate monochrome direction may use long fashionable silhouettes, differentiated angular faces, high-contrast ink, large black shapes, dramatic negative space and restrained gothic motifs. It can draw on broad qualities of stylish supernatural manga without copying a specific artist's line.

## Male facial differentiation

Finn uses softened square shapes: a wider rounded jaw, open expressive eyes, thicker straighter eyebrows, a shorter nose, a broad expressive mouth, warmer coloring and a direct head position.

Chaz uses triangles and verticals: a longer narrow face, pointed jaw, high cheekbones, narrow half-lidded eyes, thin raised eyebrows, a longer straight nose, a restrained asymmetrical mouth, pale coloring and a raised chin.

Sirius uses smaller compressed shapes, a hesitant posture and an inward visual rhythm.

Blaise’s selected hair accessory is the black blushing spider clip (round 3, option 20). Spider pendants are removed from all outfits. Her academic polo and blazer uniforms have no spider/web embroidery, web lace, spider pins or spider ribbon charms; the hair clip remains. Casual clothing may retain its web fashion details.
