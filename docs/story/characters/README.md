## Missing characters (in order of urgency)

1. **The card giver** — mentioned but not placed. The natural candidate is the shopkeeper, which gives him a real role rather than a decorative one.
2. **The first ZAPS user** — an ordinary, likeable student who destroys himself in front of Fynn. Makes the threat concrete. Ideally someone Fynn has beaten, who is trying to close the gap.
3. **The middleman dealer** — a student who resells inside the school. The real face of the problem, well before the boss.
4. **The bully** — the one who harasses the nerd. Needed from act 1 on, and perfect for a multi-against-one duel.
5. **The witness to Fynn's cheating** — someone who knows. Turns his guilt into a narrative threat.
6. **A fallen mentor** — a formerly brilliant duelist who fell for having cheated. Embodies the thesis before Fynn understands it. One of the teachers can be him.

## Additional leads

- A ZAPS adult infiltrated into the academy (sponsor, coach, recruiter)
- A secondary rival, honest and hard-working — a direct contrast with lazy Fynn
- The user's sister / friend, who comes asking for help

## On the girl of the trio

The question is not "who is she" but **"which premise is missing?"**. If the nerd embodies intellectual effort and Fynn untrained talent, the empty place is the **second-hander's**: someone gifted who plays for the approval of others, not for themselves. She would then be the mirror of chapter 2.
