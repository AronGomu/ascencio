# Qualification Arc — scenario

> Status: defined

## Purpose

Introduce Finn, Violet Academy, basic duel mechanics, future cast, academy environment and map access.

## Beats

- Finn arrives at Violet Academy.
- Examiner conducts a fully playable entrance qualification duel.
- The duel is a single game, not scripted.
- Finn may win or lose; loss does not cause game over or block admission.
- Spectators react to the actual result: Sirius, Alexis, Chaz, Zen, Analytical Student, teacher or examiner and Dean may appear.
- Zen is introduced subtly as the academy's best duelist.
- Finn is admitted.
- Full academy map opens; Free Duel remains unavailable.

## Assets

Qualification arena, Finn and every appearing character's complete current outfit set, tutorial overlays, VN interface, stadium ambience, duel music, VN music and UI sounds.
