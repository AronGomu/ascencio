# Superseded Chapter 1 cheating plot

> Status: historical archive
> Superseded by updated Chapter 1 canon supplied in `ascensio_chapter1_story_gameplay_canon.md`.

This document preserves removed Chapter 1 decisions so their history remains visible.

## Removed main plot — ZAPS

A large corporation was planned to enter schools with a product that let students cheat or become better for payment, at the cost of their health. The mechanism was not yet defined.

Fynn was planned to cheat before ZAPS entered the story. After a revelation, he would fight ZAPS because it was unjust and destructive to mental health.

The planned thesis was: cheating does not pay, morally or practically; fraud harms the person who commits it.

## Removed Fynn cheating arc

The planned pivot contained two cheats:

1. Fynn cheated, won and felt bad without understanding why.
2. Fynn cheated and lost anyway, becoming devastated.

The planned revelation was that everyone progressed while Fynn stagnated. The cheating arc was tied to an impossible choice where cheating was his only way to win.

## Removed ZAPS cast

The previous roster included:

- ZAPS boss as background antagonist.
- First ZAPS user: an ordinary, likeable student who destroyed himself while trying to close the gap.
- Middleman dealer: a student reselling ZAPS inside school.
- Adult ZAPS infiltrator: sponsor, coach or recruiter.
- Witness to Fynn's cheating.
- Fallen mentor: formerly brilliant duelist who fell after cheating.

## Removed Chapter 1 duel target

The previous Chapter 1 target was at least 20 key duels. The proposed gap-filling plan used six to eight additional opponents or rematches, including a rival rematch, two Senior encounters and a bully strengthened by ZAPS.

## Current replacement

The updated canon removes cheating as Chapter 1 plot, thesis and central moral structure. Fynn's flaw remains laziness, impulsiveness, lack of discipline and long-term growth. Chapter 1 now centers on academy life, the Nerd Arc, Girl Arc, Rival arcs, Transfer Student Arc, Missing Brother Arc and Senior Finale Arc.
