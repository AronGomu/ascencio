ASCENCIO — brand renditions

Four renditions of one pinned world: dark ground, translucent glass panels, rectangular/angular geometry, angelic golden-white identity — gold primary, white secondary — classy serif type. They differ only where the brief left an axis free: warmth of the black, density of the glass, corner language, and serif voice. Each is applied to the same four surfaces — logo, main menu, duel HUD, story dialogue.

VARIANT A

Gilded Reliquary

Cinzel · EB Garamond

Warm near-black, like a dim chapel lit from above. Glass panels are deep-blurred and chamfered at 45° — every panel reads as a cut gold-edged plate. The logo is engraved metal: Roman inscriptional capitals with a gold-to-white vertical polish.

Pick when the game should feel like a sacred object — richest gold presence of the four, most ornamental, most "angelic" in the literal sense.

VARIANT B

Obsidian Standard

Marcellus · Spectral

Cold graphite black, square corners, hairline borders — the closest to a modern Linux desktop (thin frosted panels, list-style menu, crisp 1px lines). White carries the interface; gold appears only on the logo's initial, the primary action, and phase state. The most disciplined and the most "operable".

Pick when long duel sessions matter most — quietest surface, gold stays rare so it keeps meaning. Least ornamental, most tool-grade.

VARIANT C

Aurea Lux

Bodoni Moda · EB Garamond

Absolute black with a golden dawn rising from below — the ascension made literal. Panels are glass lit by that dawn, with gradient-gold border light that falls off toward the top. The logo glows rather than shines: high-contrast didone capitals wrapped in halo light. The primary action is the only solid-gold object on screen.

Pick when the story's register should dominate — most cinematic and atmospheric, strongest first impression, needs the most care to keep duel screens legible.

VARIANT D

Basilica Slate

Forum · Source Serif 4

Blue-black slate — the one rendition that keeps kinship with the shipped duel palette (#08101f family), so migration is a restyle, not a repaint. Frosted blue-tinted glass, shallow chamfers, gold used strictly as the ceremonial color: logo warmth, primary action, phase marker.

Pick when continuity with the current build matters — cheapest to adopt across all three domains, calm, still unmistakably the pinned brand.
