# Explanation from reviewer B for prospective refinement execution

## 1. Outcome and scope

Given a required skill-folder path, I would produce a complete, readable sibling candidate preserving the source’s intent, essential behavior, dependencies and literal outputs. Optional priorities, iteration count, destination and review context configure the work. Success requires completing the requested iterations, resolving comprehension disagreements, validating relevant changes and delivering external evidence. The source remains read-only; replacement, installation, promotion and commits are outside this process.

## 2. Process overview

I would establish the behavioral baseline and incoming explanation, simplify the candidate, then alternate verification and three fresh comprehension reviews until misunderstandings are resolved. Each completed iteration records measurements and findings before the next simplification. Linked authoring guidance governs preservation audits and boundary checks. Its automatic-review requirement does not recursively invoke refinement for candidate changes or supporting artifacts within this review.
