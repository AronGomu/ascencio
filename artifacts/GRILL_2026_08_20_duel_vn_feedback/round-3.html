Where the decks live and what the app opens on

Eight decisions. Your answers turned a feedback round into a restructure: the story save now owns decks as well as cards, the story gates deck building by ownership, and the visual novel’s title screen becomes the app’s main menu. Each of those has exactly one thing I refuse to guess.

1. Where do a story save’s decks physically live?

The collection is already inside StoryState, so it is per-save and rolls back with a load for free. Decks are in a separate database with history, favourites and a default pointer — whether they follow the same rollback rule is a real semantic choice, not a storage detail.

2. What does a brand-new story save start with?

If story duels use your own decks and building is ownership-gated, a fresh save with an empty collection cannot duel at all. The first encounter has to be reachable.

3. Which deck does a story duel actually use?

Story encounters currently hand the shell an encounter id and the duel resolves bundled preset decks. Once a save owns decks, the pre-battle screen has to say which one is going in.

4. In story mode, how does the catalog treat cards you do not own?

You said the collection screen gets a “show every existing card” checkbox, default off, and that in story mode you build only from owned cards. The catalog inside the editor needs the same rule spelled out — and the two surfaces can share one setting or keep separate ones.

5. How is “free play owns everything at max copies” represented?

Writing a real record of every card times three into storage would be a five-figure map maintained on every build; a mode flag the ownership check reads is the same behaviour with none of the storage.

6. Main menu is the story title screen — what happens to routes and lazy loading?

The shell loads each domain on demand (domain-loaders.ts) and build:verify enforces a byte budget per domain chunk. Making the story’s title the app root either pulls the whole story domain into the first paint, or splits the title screen out of it.

7. What sits behind “Start a match” in free play?

Free play’s match entry is today’s Duel Simulator, which already picks your deck and pins a fixed Shaddoll opponent. The question is how much of that changes in this round.

8. One plan, or two sequential plans?

Counting what is now settled: 10 duel items plus the encoder audit and the rewind, 12 VN shop/reveal items, the set-image pipeline, the collection screen, save-owned decks with a migration, the ownership gate, and the menu restructure. That is roughly 35–40 commit-sized tickets in one file tree.
