# Explanation from reviewer 1 for iteration 3 skill comprehension

## 1. Outcome and scope

Improve one supplied target AI package through reproducible paired evaluations against its permanently frozen starting AI and any supplied additional opponents. Owner limits are optional overrides. Success means completing the planned decisions and their analyses, checking the evidence, ranking existing comparable versions, and exporting the final accepted source with a checked report. Only target AI behavior changes; decks, engine, legality and hidden-information boundaries remain fixed. Publishing, merging and pushing require their separate authorizations. This explanation uses only the refined package and explanation template; it does not execute the improvement workflow or consult its external references.

## 2. Process overview

Prepare frozen identities, limits, schedule and separately calibrated match/replay pools; evaluate baseline, then evaluate combined candidates. Decide before dispatching loss-only slice analysts. The orchestrator merges diagnosis, selects ordinary additions or regression repairs, implements isolated variants, validates every eligible reason by replay, and combines passing edits for the next evaluation. Repeat until completed decisions, no validated edit, or a stop condition. Resource exhaustion preserves an incomplete checkpoint; only eligible completed work reaches ranking, export and closure cleanup.
