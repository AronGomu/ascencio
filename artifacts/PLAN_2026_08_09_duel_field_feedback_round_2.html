Decisions taken

Four were the user's call. The rest are logged assumptions in the markdown index.

QuestionAnswerCost
Deck reveals — item 13 tail. Worker tracks a count, not a list. Full deck order tracking now. T9: new contract field, projector state, validator, 8 fixture files.
"Auto-validate all the system hints" — what counts? Empty chains + non-decision prompts (single-option option, single-position selectPosition, forced one-choice chain). T4: one pure function, nine unit cases.
Avatars — no character art exists. Reuse the bundled card back. T1: avatarUrl prop so real art swaps in later with no layout change.
Graveyard/banished/deck/extra as targets. Map them to stack targets in card-mapping.ts. T6: widened resolution union; kills the chain modal at the root.

The sharp one is deck reveals. The obvious model — mirror list_main, index by the engine's deck sequence — needs a fact we refuse to take on faith: whether sequence 0 is the top or the bottom. Wrong guess does not crash; it quietly shows the wrong card at the wrong position. So the projector stores offsets from the top and only writes them from three messages that already speak in offsets: 

DRAW              → shift every reveal down by drawn.length
CONFIRM_DECKTOP   → cards[i] is the i-th from the top
DECK_TOP          → count IS the offset from the top

SHUFFLE_DECK | SWAP_GRAVE_DECK | REVERSE_DECK | any MOVE touching a deck
                  → clear every reveal for that player

The asymmetry is the safety property: the projection may forget a reveal. It can never invent one. Any future change must preserve that. See ADR-008.

Board geometry — where an overlay may live

The previous overhaul lost a session to a field bar painted over card targets, swallowing 344 retried Playwright clicks. The board has exactly one free horizontal band.

Strip container is pointer-events: none. Only a chip that is currently a <button> gets pointer-events: auto. Centring the strip on y 50% would overlap p1:mainMonster:1 — that is the exact failure mode from last time.

Risks worth naming

  • Overlay pointer interception (T3, T8, T11). Every new field overlay must stay in the free band or outside zone extents, container pointer-events: none. The chromium responsive-composition spec is the gate. Never relax it — it asserts the right thing.
  • Ordering trap in T6. stackChoices must not count toward fieldCapable until T8 ships the list dialog. Flip it early and a graveyard activation becomes unanswerable: modal hidden, field inert, duel deadlocked.
  • Empty-response trap in T5. validatePromptSelection rejects an empty selection for single-family prompts even when cancelable. Chain prompts are exactly that. Outside-click cancel is gated on family; T11 gives chains a pass.
  • Visibility foot-gun in T10. isCardIdentityVisible(0, 0, "deck", …) returns true for your own deck — it would reveal the whole thing. Deck entries read faceUp && code instead, with a comment at the call site.
  • Fixture blast radius in T9. The contract validator asserts deck.length === deckCount, so eight fixture files need a deck. A deckSlots(player, count) helper absorbs it.
