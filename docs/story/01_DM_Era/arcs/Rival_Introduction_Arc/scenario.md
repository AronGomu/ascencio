# Chaz Introduction Arc — scenario

> Status: defined at high level; order undecided

## Purpose

Introduce status conflict and establish Chaz as more than a generic bully.

## Beats

- Chaz Goon A challenges Finn in a single-game duel; either result continues the story.
- If Goon A loses, he reports the result to Chaz.
- Chaz Goon B challenges Finn, dismissing him as "just a Red" and blaming Goon A's incompetence.
- A public incident humiliates, mistreats or unfairly dismisses a Red student.
- Finn intervenes because he hates obvious injustice.
- Chaz reacts according to the prior goon results but confronts Finn in every branch.
- Finn wins a mandatory Best of 3 against Chaz.
- Chaz rationalizes the defeat as luck, matchup, underestimation or preparation failure.

## Branch invariant

The public incident and Chaz confrontation work after 2–0, 1–1 or 0–2 goon results. Dialogue changes; progression does not.
