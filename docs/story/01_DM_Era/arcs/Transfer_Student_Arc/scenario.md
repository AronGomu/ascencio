# Blaise Arc — childhood friend arrives

> Status: planned; around December

## Purpose

Bring Finn's energetic childhood friend into Violet Academy and establish the gap between her public explanations and romantic motive.

## Beats

- Blaise arrives as a new adult student around December.
- Her parents previously prevented her from choosing Violet Academy and a duelist or conflict-profession path.
- She challenged that decision and changed her education to rejoin Finn.
- She provides plausible practical reasons for the change without admitting its emotional center.
- She inserts herself into Finn's training, organization and social plans with bright, mischievous energy.
- She shows interest in the group's investigation while claiming curiosity or practicality as her reason.
- Finn does not recognize that she is in love with him. Blaise does not fully admit it to herself.

## Open continuity

Her definitive name, public explanation, deck, exact relationships with Alexis and Sirius, and place in the investigation remain open.
