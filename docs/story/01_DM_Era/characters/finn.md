# Finn — protagonist

> Status: mostly defined for Chapter 1

## Identity

- Name: Finn.
- Role: protagonist and player-controlled duelist.
- Academic stage: adult first-year Violet Academy student.
- Rank: Red at the beginning of Chapter 1.
- Family: present but off-screen in Chapter 1.
- Physique: athletic but approachable.

## Backstory

Finn has ordinary middle-class origins and ordinary schooling. His parents once forced him to study something else and forbade dueling for one or two months because duelists were poorly regarded. He concluded that he should never again do anything except what he feels like doing.

## Personality

Finn is energetic, hot-headed and hungry for duels. He is lazy outside his genuine interests and does only enough academic work to pass. He intervenes when he sees obvious injustice. He is a defined character rather than a self-insert.

## Motivation

- Desire: become the best duelist in the world because he genuinely loves Duel Monsters.
- Weakness: impulsiveness, lack of discipline and neglect of anything that does not interest him.
- Need: understand that chosen effort in service of a value is not parental constraint.
- Chapter development: begin taking responsibility for the work needed to reach his goal.

What Finn calls egoism is whim-worship: following immediate impulse instead of rationally serving a long-term value. His laziness harms his own progress, not primarily other people.

## Appearance

Finn has dark auburn, slightly disordered hair; a softened square face; frank expressive eyes; relatively thick eyebrows; and a warm, confident expression. His open posture suggests someone ready to step in. He wears the simple canonical Red uniform described in [`../visual-direction.md`](../visual-direction.md), not the elaborate long coat from early visual experiments.

## Relationships

- Sirius: first official friend after Sirius's arc.
- Alexis: investigation partner after her first arc.
- Blaise: childhood friend whose romantic motive he does not recognize.
- Chaz: status-based competitive opponent.
- Zen: Violet Academy benchmark and likely final opponent.

## Duel identity

Finn uses the player's deck. No fixed archetype is required; adaptability justifies this in fiction. A future character gives him an iconic spirit card, which is not mandatory in the deck.

He reacts strongly to unjust humiliation. A clean, clear defeat makes him depressed. Pride and a few material goods are suitable Chapter 1 stakes.
