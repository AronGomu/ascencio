# Explanation from reviewer-2 for refined skill execution comprehension

## 1. Outcome and scope

Given a target AI package, optional opponent packages and owner limits, I would seek a strictly better paired-game win rate through target-only changes, preserving decks, engine, legality and information boundaries. Successful completion means finishing planned work (normally 50 candidate decisions with their analyses), or the explicit no-validated-candidate stopping branch, then verifying, ranking existing results, exporting the accepted source and delivering checked compact evidence. Suspension is incomplete, resumable work.

## 2. Process overview

I would freeze identities, schedule and limits; validate and calibrate; evaluate the baseline; then cycle through evaluation, decision, delegated loss diagnosis, isolated replay validation and combined candidate preparation. Accepted-source additions are the ordinary branch; a measured regression instead triggers removal/update repairs based on that regressing source. Every evaluation receives analysis after its decision, including the last or rejected evaluation. Resource exhaustion interrupts this sequence with a checkpoint, without final ranking, export or cleanup.
