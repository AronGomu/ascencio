# Explanation from review-a for pending-rule skill comprehension

## 1. Outcome and scope

Improve one required target AI against optional additional opponents plus its frozen starting self-opponent, returning accepted source, verified paired results and compact history even when no edit qualifies. Owner limits and a compatible checkpoint are optional inputs. Only target AI may change; engine, content, decks, legality and information boundaries remain protected. This explanation reads the complete pending package and explanation template only; no campaign was executed or package edited.

## 2. Process overview

Prepare frozen identities, budgets, analysis policy and both calibrations; evaluate baseline, decide, then analyze. Subsequent isolated fixes or regression repairs must pass current-source replay before assembly and paired evaluation. Every evaluated candidate is decided and analyzed, including the last. Continue until the decision ceiling or no qualifying edit; suspend on resource or stop incidents. Pending rules survive below-threshold outcomes with accumulated evidence, while only a complete valid 300-case failure establishes terminal discard.
