3. Translation — the part that is not mechanical

English canon means I translate, and translation is interpretation. Two safeguards:

Safeguard 1 — the French original stays recoverable. Commit 1 imports it verbatim, unsplit. git show <sha>:docs/story/scenario-fangame-bible.fr.md returns the authored text forever.

Safeguard 2 — the source terms were already English. The bible translates Egri/Truby/McKee/Rand vocabulary into French; English canon restores the originals rather than inventing wording:
