# Chapter 1 — Duel Monsters

> Status: working draft — in design. Era mechanics: Fusion, Ritual.

**Setting:** a duel academy. A gentle introduction. Fynn enters first year (~15-16 years old) like everyone else.

**Arc:**

- He makes a name for himself over the year, and builds a circle of friends
- The cheating arc (see [`../characters/fynn.md`](../characters/fynn.md))
- A series of defeats → the revelation
- **Climax:** a duel against the school's best student, a third-year who is leaving the academy — a nod to *Judai vs Kaiser Ryo* in GX. This is the finale, **not the main plot**.
- **Exit:** Fynn has become famous

## Main plot — ZAPS

A **large corporation** works its way into the schools to introduce a product that lets students cheat / be better, for payment — **at the cost of their health**. (Not exactly a drug; the precise mechanism is still to be defined.)

Fynn cheats **before** ZAPS is fully introduced into the story. After his revelation he wants to fight ZAPS: he understands that it is unjust and destructive to people's mental health.

**Thesis:** teach people not to cheat, because it will harm them afterwards. Defrauding is not virtuous.

## Gameplay target

**Target: at least 20 key duels in chapter 1.**

The current casting supplies about 12. Two ways to close the gap:

- 6-8 additional functional opponents
- **Better: rematches.** The rival beaten and then lost to, the senior faced twice, the bully met again stronger thanks to ZAPS. An opponent seen again is always more dramatic than a new one.
