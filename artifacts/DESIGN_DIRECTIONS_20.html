Where these came from

The canon does the work. Five facts in docs/story/ are unusually generous to a designer, and every direction below is traceable to at least one of them.

Objectivist foundation

  • Rand at every level, aesthetics included — 1930s American industrial modernism is the movement's native look
  • Value, trade, earned achievement: ledgers, securities, engraved instruments of value
  • The duel is character and rational choice, never fate

Hard magic, studiable by reason

  • Fixed knowable laws with explicit costs — technical documentation, not mysticism
  • Whoever treats magic as unfathomable is wrong inside the fiction
  • Patent drawings, scientific plates, spectra, lab notation

The epistemological duel

  • Cheating is legal until proven by binding logical argument
  • A failed accusation costs information and reputation
  • Proof trees, evidence boards, courtroom record, audit

A god who banned violence

  • Every conflict resolved by play, by decree, from the background
  • Canon law, illuminated codex, stone tablet, liturgy
  • Spirits imprinted into manufactured cards — talismans, shrine paper

Chapter 1 is a school; chapter = era

  • Academy year, first-years, club noticeboards, gym, exams, seniors
  • ZAPS: a corporation selling performance at the cost of health
  • Six eras, six graphic languages, one shell — the structure hands you a mechanic

Stated tone references

  • Spirit Caller, Nightmare Troubadour — dual-screen handheld UI
  • The Duelists of the Roses — painted tactical board
  • World Championship — dense stat panels
  • Duelists are badly regarded: the underground fight bill, the back-room market

Excluded before the list was written — the rut

  • Near-black with one neon accent and glowing edges. This is where every AI-built card game lands, and it is also what the repo currently ships (#08101f ground, #73daca glow). It appears once at the bottom, played straight, as the standing exit.
  • Warm cream ground, high-contrast display serif, terracotta accent. Directions 02 and 11 pass near this and are pushed off it deliberately — stamped vermilion and mechanical hatch in one, lapis-and-gold drench in the other.
  • Broadsheet editorial: hairline rules, italic display serif, small tracked mono labels. Not represented. It is a magazine's look, not a duel's.
  • Egyptian pastiche. The canon says 100% original, no link to the official universe. Millennium items, hieroglyph borders and sand palettes are borrowed identity, and would be the one thing a fan game must not borrow.

How to read a card

Every card carries the same anatomy so nothing wins on presentation: thesis and what it refuses · canon tie, the specific story fact it serves · palette with real hex values · type, the intended commercial face and the local proxy actually rendering here · three live mockups, duel field, VN scene, deck editor, built from the same markup skeleton repainted by that direction's tokens · signature interaction, motion, honest risk, and where the world is strongest.

The mockups are structural, not final art — they show composition, material and colour at a glance, at the size where a direction either reads or does not. Fonts render in locally installed proxies because this file fetches nothing; each type block names the face the build would actually license.

The standing exit — always available, never recommended

00 · The incumbent, played straight

Deep navy ground, teal accent, gold selection, soft glow on the field: the palette in src/styles/tokens.css today. It is the category standard and it works. Taking this door means committing to it properly rather than half-replacing it — naming two or three shipped card games as the craft bar, and executing that convention at full fidelity across all three domains, with no smuggled quirk from the twenty above.

What happens next

  1. Pick one — or pick a lead plus one you want it raised by. Twenty is a hand to choose from, not a menu to blend; a build that splits the difference between two worlds ships neither.
  2. PRODUCT.md is missing, so the dice stayed in the cup. concept-seed.mjs exits NO_PRODUCT_MD without it — the roll that stops a build converging on the safest concept could not run. Twenty directions spanning eleven material families is the opposite failure mode from the one the dice guard against, so nothing here is invalidated, but the direction round proper wants an init pass first.
  3. Then the build contract. Locked direction → DESIGN.md written from the built world → tokens replace src/styles/tokens.css wholesale, since every domain already resolves colour from that one file.
  4. Scope note. Each direction is costed on the effort meter for all three domains together. Anything at four or five bars needs authored assets — plates, textures, sign paint, painted portraits — and is a content project as much as a CSS one.
