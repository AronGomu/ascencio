# Sirius — analyst and friend

> Status: provisional name; strongly defined for Chapter 1

## Identity

- Role: Finn's main friend and central trio member.
- Family: younger brother of Zen, who is two years older.
- Rank: Yellow in the current visual direction.
- Physique: noticeably shorter and thinner than Finn in Chapter 1.

## Backstory

Zen has always been confident, decisive, capable and reliable under pressure. He handled everything while Sirius grew up in his shadow. Sirius does not deeply resent him; constant comparison damaged his confidence.

## Personality

Sirius is insecure, avoidant and curious. He avoids confrontation, delays difficult decisions, lets more capable people act and escapes situations exposing weakness. Unanswered questions bother him. He investigates people, decks and mysteries because he wants information, not automatically from courage or altruism.

Sirius explores possibilities and understands systems. Alexis eliminates possibilities until she reaches the facts. He may accumulate information from pure curiosity; she prioritizes evidence that advances her investigation.

## Appearance

Sirius has disordered blue-black hair, a young narrow face and intelligent eyes. His slightly closed shoulders, hand near his face and habit of holding notebooks, files or a deck case communicate hesitation and observation. He must look reserved without appearing insignificant; sustained attention reveals concentration and hidden competence.

## Duel identity

Sirius is not an exceptional pilot. Under pressure he hesitates, overthinks, makes avoidable mistakes and often sees the correct line too late. His talent is metagame understanding: deck building, card evaluation, matchup theory, statistics, prediction, analysis and identifying weaknesses.

> Zen: Give me a deck and I'll pilot it better than you.
>
> Sirius: Give me a format and I'll understand it better than you.

## Chapter development

Three students take a meaningful card after defeating Sirius. Finn intervenes. Sirius later hides in his room after missing class, and Finn forces him to act through a Best of 3 duel. He returns to class and becomes Finn's friend without becoming confident overnight.

Long-term development: a Chapter 2 growth spurt makes him taller than Finn while his internal identity remains small. Later he becomes a better duelist and values theory, construction and analysis without measuring himself against Zen.
