# Transfer Student Arc — scenario

> Status: planned; around December

## Purpose

Introduce Fynn's childhood friend as a permanent Duel Academy student and add a hidden romantic motivation.

## Beats

- Fynn's childhood friend transfers from her conventional high school.
- Her parents previously refused Duel Academy because they rejected a duelist or warrior-style career for her.
- Separation from Fynn made her increasingly unhappy.
- She forced the issue; her parents accepted the transfer.
- She publicly gives another reason for transferring.
- Her actual reason is being near Fynn because she is secretly in love with him.
- Fynn does not know this. The player may initially remain unaware.

## Open continuity

Her name, personality beyond romantic motivation, deck, relationships with Girl and Nerd and romance foregrounding remain undefined.
